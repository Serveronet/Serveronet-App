## P2P Websites
`Free and uncensorable`

Decentralized Cryptographic Peer-to-Peer Network of Sites.
Serveronet revolutionizes web hosting by eliminating the reliance on central servers or traditional domain registries. By forming a distributed peer network, it ensures that your online presence is inherently resilient to single points of failure, censorship, and takedown attempts. This foundational architecture guarantees maximum uptime and freedom for content creators globally.

## Uncensorable
`No central server. No domains.`

The platform’s core strength lies in its lack of mandatory centralized infrastructure. Unlike conventional web services that require registered domains (which can be seized or blocked), Serveronet's approach is entirely decentralized. While optional OpenNIC domains allow users to maintain a clear-net DNS fallback, the primary existence of the site relies solely on peer connectivity, making it extremely difficult for any single entity to deplatform or take away your domain authority.

## Free and Open source
`Hosted by peers`

By publishing your site directly to participating peers, Serveronet creates an intrinsically resilient hosting model. Your content remains online indefinitely as long as the network finds interested hosts. Furthermore, the entire framework is built with a commitment to open standards; it exclusively utilizes scripting languages, avoiding proprietary "black box" binary blobs. This ensures that every building block is transparently auditable and accessible for community contribution.

## Decentralized
`PQ Crystals cryptography, BitTorrent Trackers, IPFS`

Serveronet adopts a multi-layered decentralized approach to ensure maximum reliability and robustness. It leverages sophisticated technologies including Post-Quantum (PQ) Crystal cryptography for security, integrates reliable BitTorrent trackers for content location, and optionally utilizes the InterPlanetary File System (IPFS) as an advanced layer of file storage. This combination provides a resilient, distributed backbone for all site data transmission.

## Anonymity
`Tor and public keys`

User identity and communication on Serveronet are secured through cutting-edge cryptography. The platform relies on PQ Crystals cryptography to generate unique digital identities without requiring traditional personal identifiers like email addresses. For enhanced privacy, users also have the option to route all their network communications through the Tor anonymity network, maximizing both security and user discretion.

## SEO Friendly
`Lives in clearnet`

A significant differentiator for Serveronet is its ability to operate seamlessly within conventional internet visibility. Unlike purely Darknet P2P networks, Serveronet sites are designed to behave like regular, legitimate websites. This compatibility means that standard web tools—including Ads, CDNs (Content Delivery Networks), and traditional search engine crawlers are fully supported, ensuring high discoverability and integration with the established clearnet ecosystem.

## Developer friendly
`P2P Site Development made easy`

The platform is designed for ease of use by developers without requiring specialized knowledge of obscure P2P networking protocols. Serveronet adheres to widely recognized web development patterns, supporting standard frontend frameworks, robust backend services, and familiar database/user account structures. This means that much of the complex peer-to-peer negotiation and data handling occurs "under the hood," allowing developers to focus on building features rather than managing network complexity.

## Deployment
`Linux, Windows, Servers`

Serveronet offers unparalleled deployment flexibility, making it accessible to a wide range of users and technical setups. You can run it locally as a client on your own personal PC, or you can deploy the service onto public hosting servers. The system has been engineered for simplicity, ensuring compatibility with common environments such as Shared Hosting platforms, Virtual Private Servers (VPS), Raspberry Pi OS (Raspbian), and many more.

## Moderation and Control
`Optional governance`

While promoting freedom, Serveronet does recognize the need for community safety and order. The architecture provides mechanisms for optional governance through moderation capabilities. Site owners are empowered to actively moderate content, preventing unwanted material from being published on their domains. Additionally, client owners retain the ability to ban specific sites to maintain a healthy hosting environment.
